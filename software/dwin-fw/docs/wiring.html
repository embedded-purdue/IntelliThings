Companion Display Wiring

DWIN DMG80480T050_09WN display, Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47, FNIRSI bench supply, and the optional 24-LED bar. The display runs from the bench supply; the ESP32 runs from your laptop's USB-C. They share only ground and two serial wires.

Power-up order

  1. Supply output off. Set 12.0 V and a 0.5 A current limit (OVP 14 V, OCP 0.8 A if your model has them).
  2. Check the TTL pads next to the connector are bridged.
  3. Connect only the supply: red to pins 1–2, black to pin 8. Identify the cable's wires with a continuity test first.
  4. Output on. Expect about 0.1–0.25 A and the backlight on. If the supply hits its current limit, turn it off and recheck polarity.
  5. Measure pin 4 against pin 7: a positive reading (3.3–5 V) means TTL. A negative reading means RS232; stop there.
  6. Connect the ESP32: GND to pin 7, GP18 to pin 5, and pin 4 through the 1 kΩ / 2 kΩ divider to GP19 (it measured +5 V). Then plug its USB-C into the laptop.
  7. Optional LED strip, with the ESP32's USB unplugged: GND first, then 5V, then DIN through 330 Ω. When unplugging, take GND off last.

Never

  • Connect the supply's 12 V to any ESP32 pin.
  • Power the display from USB or the ESP32's 5V pin: the display needs at least 6 V.
  • Wire the ESP32 to the display while it is in RS232 mode (pads open).
  • Connect the display's TX2 (5 V) straight to an ESP32 pin; always through the divider.
  • Run the LED strip without its GND wire, or move its wires while the ESP32 is powered.
